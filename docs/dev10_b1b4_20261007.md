# Dev10 B1-B4 Developmental Results (2026-10-07)

## Scope and provenance

This is a developmental engineering/ablation round, not confirmatory evidence.
The frozen dev10 split contains 5 real and 5 synthetic cases. Runs used the
locally available Qwen3-VL-2B-Instruct model, native Blender, and the official
4DCodeBench checker/scorer at benchmark revision
`aa05fb426273b59f47fac71825f77ca1b7a64c08`. Per-case metrics and dispositions
are recorded in `results.tsv`; raw generated scenes and run logs remain on the
experiment host and are not part of this repository update.

## Runs and validity

- `dev10-b1b4-20261007T163230Z`: B1 and B2, plus the initial B3/B4 attempt.
  B1 passed the official checker on 0/10 cases. B2 passed build, checker, and
  scorer on 10/10. The initial B3/B4 used a world-to-camera transform where the
  compiler contract is camera-to-world; both are marked `keep=reject` and must
  not be interpreted as method results.
- `dev10-b3b4-gaugefix-20261007T172508Z`: interrupted diagnostic attempt; no
  complete result rows are accepted.
- `dev10-b3b4-c2w-v2-20261007T172722Z`: corrected B3/B4 replay using the exact
  B2 scenes from the first run. Both arms passed build, checker, and official
  scoring on 10/10 cases. The gauge/projection convention was corrected and
  covered by unit tests.

The B2-versus-B3 replay preserved `dynamic_iou` exactly; maximum absolute
differences were approximately `3.5e-7` for flow distribution and `6.23e-6`
for EMD, with `track2d_dtw` identical. This supports implementation-level
projection preservation, not a performance improvement claim.

## B4 trade-off versus B2

Means over the five cases with the relevant metric available:

| Metric | B2 | B4 | Direction |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| `dynamic_iou` | 0.039742 | 0.016129 | higher is better; B4 worse |
| `flow_distribution` | 0.151474 | 0.051263 | higher is better; B4 worse |
| `track2d_dtw` | 0.000721 | 0.006752 | higher is better; B4 better |
| `trajectory_dtw` | 0.193882 | 0.118757 | higher is better; B4 worse |
| `emd_step` | 0.087333 | 0.038248 | higher is better; B4 worse |
| `scene_3d` | 0.774220 | 0.774218 | effectively unchanged |

All metrics in this table are higher-is-better under the pinned official scorer. The corrected reading is that B4 is lower on Dynamic IoU, Flow, Trajectory DTW, and EMD; higher on Track2D; and effectively tied on Scene 3D. These mixed outcomes do not establish that CEM helps overall. The B1 failure,
small metric-specific sample counts, and reuse of B2 scenes in the corrected
B3/B4 replay mean a prospective, fully-valid paired comparison is still needed.
Next: fix direct-generation schema/camera output, then rerun B1-B4 under one
frozen protocol before adding further ideas.
